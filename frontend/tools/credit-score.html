Habits that lift your CIBIL score

Pay on time

Never miss an EMI or credit card due date. Payment history accounts for 35% of your score.

Use under 30%

Keep your total credit-card utilisation below 30% of your sanction limit.

Avoid multiple hard enquiries

Comparing on MyCashBridge is a soft inquiry. Avoid applying to 10 banks simultaneously.

Check for errors

Regularly check your report for settled accounts marked wrong and dispute them early.
